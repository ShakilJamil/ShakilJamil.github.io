Liberation's Canvas: A Graphic Tale of Freedom's War

Original 3,400-line C++ OpenGL (GLUT) program compiled for the browser — depicting freedom fighters defending the riverbanks, armed bunker warfare, military tanks, naval warships, and day/night ambient lighting.

Speed:
Day View Active [D] Sound Active 60 FPS • Native OpenGL Engine
Press D for Day • Press N for Night • Press P for Proposal
01

Armed Combat & Flag of Freedom

Features freedom fighters in bunkers with rifles, animated bullet trajectories (position4), casualty effects, and a freedom fighter proudly waving the Red & Green Bangladesh national flag.

02

Military Tanks & Naval Warships

The armed tank (car()) rolls along the road with rotating track wheels and cannon. At night, a detailed multi-tier naval warship with red keel, black hull, illuminated decks, and antenna patrols alongside a speed boat.

03

OpenGL Pipeline & Ambient Lighting

Executes the 3,400-line C++ GLUT source directly — trigonometric circle fans, matrix stack coordinate isolation (glPushMatrix), and night ambient lighting shaders (GL_LIGHT_MODEL_AMBIENT).
