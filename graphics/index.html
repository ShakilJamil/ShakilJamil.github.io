Liberation's Canvas: Dynamic Day & Night View

Interactive simulation reproducing the 3,400-line C++ OpenGL program featuring procedural floating clouds, river boat navigation, ambient lighting shifts, and rural landscape geometry.

Day Mode Active 60 FPS • Continuous Animation
Press D for Day • Press N for Night • Press P for Info
01

Trigonometric Circle Discretization

The sun, clouds, wheels, and celestial spheres are rendered by generating triangle fans across 100 angular steps using x = r * cos(θ) and y = r * sin(θ).

02

Matrix Stack Coordinate Isolation

Moving riverboats, floating cumulus clouds, and passing vehicles utilize isolated glPushMatrix() and glPopMatrix() transformations to prevent coordinate drift.

03

Dynamic Color Temperature Shifting

Transitions smoothly between daytime high-key azure lighting and nocturnal twilight ambient illumination with star field twinkling and campfire glow shaders.
